Advanced Topics in African Studies

This section outlines four advanced specialization tracks for self-directed learners who have mastered the core undergraduate curriculum. These tracks provide specialized research methodologies, advanced theoretical frameworks, and applied policy analyses across the major contemporary subfields of African Studies.

  • Critical Historiography, Archives, and Global Diasporas: Explores advanced archival deconstruction, transoceanic forced migrations, Pan-African liberation theory, and indigenous epistemologies.
  • Literary Theory, Performance, and Screen Cultures: Analyzes postcolonial poetics, oral literature and performance aesthetics, screen media ecosystems, and African feminist and queer cultural theory.
  • Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation: Investigates territorial state consolidation, agrarian and land tenure transformations, regional economic integration, and the micro-dynamics of conflict systems.
  • Environment, Critical Health, and Science & Technology Studies (African STS): Evaluates political ecology and fortress conservation, critical global health and biomedical governance, grassroots technoscience innovation, and urban infrastructural dynamics.

Critical Historiography, Archives, and Global Diasporas

This track examines the critical deconstruction of colonial archives, transoceanic forced migrations, Pan-African liberation thought, and indigenous philosophical systems.

Critical Historiography & Archival Deconstruction: Refiguring the Archive (Eds. Carolyn Hamilton et al. / Springer) - A specialized volume examining archival power, institutional erasures, and methodologies for recovering marginalized African voices from official state records.

Transoceanic Forced Migrations & Indian Ocean Diasporas: The Structure of Slavery in Indian Ocean Africa and Asia (Ed. Gwyn Campbell / Routledge) - An authoritative comparative analysis of maritime forced migration, domestic servitude, and diaspora circuits across the Indian Ocean basin.

Pan-African Intellectual History & Liberation Thought: Pan-Africanism: A History (Hakim Adi / Bloomsbury Academic) - A comprehensive historical investigation tracing the transnational organizational networks, anti-colonial congresses, and political ideologies of the global Pan-African movement.

Pan-African Intellectual History & Liberation Thought: Unity and Struggle: Speeches and Writings of Amilcar Cabral (Amílcar Cabral / Bloomsbury) - Essential revolutionary writings examining culture as an instrument of national liberation, petty-bourgeois class suicide, and peasant political mobilization.

African Philosophy & Indigenous Epistemologies: African Philosophy: Myth and Reality (Paulin J. Hountondji / Indiana University Press) - A canonical philosophical critique dismantling descriptive ethnophilosophy in favor of rigorous, open-ended, and written scientific discourse.

African Philosophy & Indigenous Epistemologies: Cultural Universals and Particulars: An African Perspective (Kwasi Wiredu / Indiana University Press) - An advanced exploration of conceptual decolonization, cross-cultural comparative philosophy, and indigenous Akan epistemology.

Literary Theory, Performance, and Screen Cultures

This track explores postcolonial and decolonial literary aesthetics, performance and oral poetics, African screen media, and contemporary feminist and queer expressive cultures.

Decolonial Poetics & African Literary Criticism: African Literature: An Anthology of Criticism and Theory (Eds. Tejumola Olaniyan & Ato Quayson / Wiley-Blackwell) - A landmark anthology compiling seminal theoretical essays on language debates, postcolonial disillusionment, structural aesthetics, and the development of the African novel.

Orature & Performance Aesthetics: Oral Literature in Africa (Ruth Finnegan / Open Book Publishers) - A comprehensive open-access monograph analyzing poetry, prose narratives, drum languages, and performance traditions across the continent.

African Cinema & Screen Media: African Cinema: Politics and Culture (Manthia Diawara / Indiana University Press) - A foundational study of anti-colonial cinema movements, production economics, state censorship, and aesthetic developments across Francophone and Anglophone African film.

African Cinema & Screen Media: Nollywood: The Creation of Nigerian Film Genres (Jonathan Haynes / University of Chicago Press) - An in-depth analysis of Nollywood's video revolution, informal distribution architectures, and popular genre conventions.

African Feminist & Queer Epistemologies: African Sexualities: A Reader (Ed. Sylvia Tamale / Pambazuka Press) - An expansive multidisciplinary reader addressing gender justice, non-normative sexualities, bodily autonomy, and legal rights through an African feminist framework.

Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation

This track investigates postcolonial state authority, agrarian land tenure transformations, continental trade integration, and the political economy of modern conflict systems.

Comparative State Formation & Territorial Power: States and Power in Africa: Comparative Lessons in Authority and Control (Jeffrey Herbst / Princeton University Press) - A comparative treatise analyzing how low population densities and severe geographical barriers shaped the broadcast of political authority and state consolidation across African history.

Agrarian Transformation & Land Sovereignty: Zimbabwe's Land Reform: Myths and Realities (Ian Scoones et al. / James Currey) - A decade-long empirical field study dismantling orthodox policy myths surrounding land redistribution, smallholder agrarian production, and rural livelihoods.

Continental Trade & Regional Integration: Assessing Regional Integration in Africa (United Nations Economic Commission for Africa) - Flagship policy reports examining intra-African trade dynamics, regional economic community convergence, and the implementation protocols of the AfCFTA.

Continental Trade & Regional Integration: Africa and International Relations in the 21st Century (Eds. Scarlett Cornelissen, Fantu Cheru, and Timothy M. Shaw / Palgrave Macmillan) - An advanced volume analyzing changing sovereignty regimes, security governance, and Africa's strategic agency in a multipolar global order.

Conflict Systems & Peacebuilding Architectures: The Real Politics of the Horn of Africa: Money, War and the Business of Power (Alex de Waal / Polity) - An analytical study explaining contemporary armed conflict and elite bargaining through the conceptual lens of the commodified political marketplace.

Conflict Systems & Peacebuilding Architectures: The Trouble with the Congo: Local Violence and the Failure of International Peacebuilding (Séverine Autesserre / Cambridge University Press) - An acclaimed field-based study examining how international peacekeeping interventions fail by systematically neglecting bottom-up local drivers of conflict.

Environment, Critical Health, and Science & Technology Studies (African STS)

This track analyzes African political ecology, critical global health governance, indigenous technoscience innovation, and urban infrastructural futures.

Political Ecology & Fortress Conservation: Imposing Wilderness: Struggles over Livelihood and Nature Preservation in Africa (Roderick P. Neumann / University of California Press) - A seminal environmental history demonstrating how the imposition of colonial conservation models dispossessed rural communities and criminalized customary resource use.

Critical Global Health & Medical Biopolitics: Improvising Medicine: An African Oncology Ward in an Emerging Cancer Epidemic (Julie Livingston / Duke University Press) - An acclaimed medical ethnography examining resource scarcity, institutional improvisation, and the social dynamics of care and palliation in an African hospital.

Science, Technology, and Society (African STS): What Do Science, Technology, and Innovation Mean from Africa? (Ed. Clapperton Chakanetsa Mavhunga / MIT Press Direct) - An open-access foundational volume redefining technoscience through African epistemologies, grassroots workshop practices, and everyday innovation.

Urban Spatial Dynamics & Infrastructure: For the City Yet to Come: Changing African Life in Four Cities (AbdouMaliq Simone / Duke University Press) - A groundbreaking urban theory introducing the concept of people as infrastructure and examining informal collaborative survival networks across African metropolises.
