# Advanced Topics in African Studies

This section outlines four advanced specialization tracks for self-directed learners who have completed the core undergraduate curriculum. The tracks cover specialized research methods, advanced theoretical frameworks, and applied policy analysis across the major contemporary subfields of African Studies.

- Critical Historiography, Archives, and Global Diasporas: Covers archival deconstruction, transoceanic forced migrations, Pan-African liberation theory, and indigenous epistemologies.
- Literary Theory, Performance, and Screen Cultures: Covers postcolonial poetics, oral literature and performance aesthetics, screen media ecosystems, and African feminist and queer cultural theory.
- Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation: Covers territorial state consolidation, agrarian and land tenure transformations, regional economic integration, and the micro-dynamics of conflict systems.
- Environment, Critical Health, and Science & Technology Studies (African STS): Covers political ecology and fortress conservation, critical global health and biomedical governance, grassroots technoscience innovation, and urban infrastructural dynamics.

## Critical Historiography, Archives, and Global Diasporas

This track examines the critical deconstruction of colonial archives, transoceanic forced migrations, Pan-African liberation thought, and indigenous philosophical systems.

Critical Historiography & Archival Deconstruction: [Refiguring the Archive (Eds. Carolyn Hamilton et al. / Springer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781402007439) - A specialized volume on archival power, institutional erasures, and methods for recovering marginalized African voices from official state records.

Transoceanic Forced Migrations & Indian Ocean Diasporas: [The Structure of Slavery in Indian Ocean Africa and Asia (Ed. Gwyn Campbell / Routledge)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780714683881) - An authoritative comparative analysis of maritime forced migration, domestic servitude, and diaspora circuits across the Indian Ocean basin.

Pan-African Intellectual History & Liberation Thought: [Pan-Africanism: A History (Hakim Adi / Bloomsbury Academic)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781474254274) - A historical study of the transnational organizational networks, anti-colonial congresses, and political ideologies of the global Pan-African movement.

Pan-African Intellectual History & Liberation Thought: [Unity and Struggle: Speeches and Writings of Amilcar Cabral (Amílcar Cabral / Bloomsbury)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781035906093) - Revolutionary writings on culture as an instrument of national liberation, petty-bourgeois class suicide, and peasant political mobilization.

African Philosophy & Indigenous Epistemologies: [African Philosophy: Myth and Reality (Paulin J. Hountondji / Indiana University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780253210968) - A philosophical critique that takes apart descriptive ethnophilosophy and argues instead for open-ended, written scientific discourse.

African Philosophy & Indigenous Epistemologies: [Cultural Universals and Particulars: An African Perspective (Kwasi Wiredu / Indiana University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780253210807) - An advanced treatment of conceptual decolonization, cross-cultural comparative philosophy, and indigenous Akan epistemology.

## Literary Theory, Performance, and Screen Cultures

This track explores postcolonial and decolonial literary aesthetics, performance and oral poetics, African screen media, and contemporary feminist and queer expressive cultures.

Decolonial Poetics & African Literary Criticism: [African Literature: An Anthology of Criticism and Theory (Eds. Tejumola Olaniyan & Ato Quayson / Wiley-Blackwell)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781405112017) - An anthology of theoretical essays on language debates, postcolonial disillusionment, structural aesthetics, and the development of the African novel.

Orature & Performance Aesthetics: [Oral Literature in Africa (Ruth Finnegan / Open Book Publishers)](https://www.openbookpublishers.com/books/10.11647/obp.0025) - An open-access monograph on poetry, prose narratives, drum languages, and performance traditions across the continent.

African Cinema & Screen Media: [African Cinema: Politics and Culture (Manthia Diawara / Indiana University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780253207074) - A foundational study of anti-colonial cinema movements, production economics, state censorship, and aesthetic developments in Francophone and Anglophone African film.

African Cinema & Screen Media: [Nollywood: The Creation of Nigerian Film Genres (Jonathan Haynes / University of Chicago Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226387956) - An in-depth analysis of Nollywood's video revolution, informal distribution systems, and popular genre conventions.

African Feminist & Queer Epistemologies: [African Sexualities: A Reader (Ed. Sylvia Tamale / Pambazuka Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780857490162) - A multidisciplinary reader on gender justice, non-normative sexualities, bodily autonomy, and legal rights from an African feminist perspective.

## Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation

This track investigates postcolonial state authority, agrarian land tenure transformations, continental trade integration, and the political economy of modern conflict systems.

Comparative State Formation & Territorial Power: [States and Power in Africa: Comparative Lessons in Authority and Control (Jeffrey Herbst / Princeton University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691164144) - A comparative study of how low population densities and severe geographical barriers shaped the projection of political authority and state consolidation across African history.

Agrarian Transformation & Land Sovereignty: [Zimbabwe's Land Reform: Myths and Realities (Ian Scoones et al. / James Currey)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781847010247) - A decade-long empirical field study that challenges orthodox policy myths about land redistribution, smallholder agricultural production, and rural livelihoods.

Continental Trade & Regional Integration: [Assessing Regional Integration in Africa (United Nations Economic Commission for Africa)](https://www.uneca.org/) - Flagship policy reports on intra-African trade dynamics, convergence among regional economic communities, and implementation protocols for the AfCFTA.

Continental Trade & Regional Integration: [Africa and International Relations in the 21st Century (Eds. Scarlett Cornelissen, Fantu Cheru, and Timothy M. Shaw / Palgrave Macmillan)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781137501301) - An advanced volume on changing sovereignty regimes, security governance, and Africa's strategic agency in a multipolar global order.

Conflict Systems & Peacebuilding Architectures: [The Real Politics of the Horn of Africa: Money, War and the Business of Power (Alex de Waal / Polity)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780745695587) - An analytical study that explains contemporary armed conflict and elite bargaining through the concept of the commodified political marketplace.

Conflict Systems & Peacebuilding Architectures: [The Trouble with the Congo: Local Violence and the Failure of International Peacebuilding (Séverine Autesserre / Cambridge University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780521156011) - A field-based study of how international peacekeeping interventions fail by systematically neglecting bottom-up local drivers of conflict.

## Environment, Critical Health, and Science & Technology Studies (African STS)

This track analyzes African political ecology, critical global health governance, indigenous technoscience innovation, and urban infrastructural futures.

Political Ecology & Fortress Conservation: [Imposing Wilderness: Struggles over Livelihood and Nature Preservation in Africa (Roderick P. Neumann / University of California Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780520234680) - An environmental history showing how colonial conservation models dispossessed rural communities and criminalized customary resource use.

Critical Global Health & Medical Biopolitics: [Improvising Medicine: An African Oncology Ward in an Emerging Cancer Epidemic (Julie Livingston / Duke University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780822353423) - A medical ethnography of resource scarcity, institutional improvisation, and the social dynamics of care and palliation in an African hospital.

Science, Technology, and Society (African STS): [What Do Science, Technology, and Innovation Mean from Africa? (Ed. Clapperton Chakanetsa Mavhunga / MIT Press Direct)](https://direct.mit.edu/books/book/3074/What-Do-Science-Technology-and-Innovation-Mean) - An open-access foundational volume that redefines technoscience through African epistemologies, grassroots workshop practices, and everyday innovation.

Urban Spatial Dynamics & Infrastructure: [For the City Yet to Come: Changing African Life in Four Cities (AbdouMaliq Simone / Duke University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780822334453) - An urban theory text that introduces the concept of people as infrastructure and examines informal collaborative survival networks across African metropolises.
