# Advanced Topics in African Studies

This section outlines four advanced specialization tracks designed for learners who have completed the core undergraduate curriculum. These tracks represent major contemporary research concentrations and methodological directions in African Studies. They may be pursued individually or combined according to research and professional interests.

- **Critical Historiography, Archives, and Global Diasporas:** Investigates the deconstruction of colonial archives, historical epistemology, transoceanic forced migrations, and Pan-African intellectual lineages.
- **Literary Theory, Performance, and Visual/Digital Cultures:** Explores postcolonial and decolonial literary aesthetics, performance and oral poetics, African screen media, and African feminist and queer thought.
- **Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation:** Examines postcolonial statehood, neopatrimonialism, agrarian transitions, resource sovereignty, regional integration, and peacebuilding architectures.
- **Environment, Critical Health, and Science/Technology Futures:** Analyzes political ecology, critical global health biopolitics, technological infrastructure and innovation (African STS), and urban spatial dynamics.

| Decolonial Poetics & African Literary Theory | How do African writers negotiate the politics of European versus indigenous languages, national allegories, and postcolonial disillusionment? ## Political Economy, Governance, and Conflict Transformation

| Subtopic / Area | Focus & Key Questions | Recommended Advanced Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State Formation, Neopatrimonialism & Governance | How have colonial administrative legacies (such as Mamdani's bifurcated state) shaped postcolonial governance, citizenship, and ethnic clientelism? What explains democratic resilience, authoritarian retrenchment, and civic mobilization in contemporary Africa? | - Mahmood Mamdani, *Citizen and Subject: Contemporary Africa and the Legacy of Late Colonialism* (Princeton University Press)<br>- Jean-François Bayart, *The State in Africa: The Politics of the Belly* (Polity)<br>- Nic Cheeseman, *Democracy in Africa: Successes, Failures, and the Struggle for Political Reform* (Cambridge University Press)<br>- [Mo Ibrahim Foundation: Ibrahim Index of African Governance](https://mo.ibrahim.foundation/iiag) (Governance metrics and datasets) |
| Agrarian Change, Resource Extraction & Unequal Exchange | How have structural adjustment programs, primary commodity dependence, and the global scramble for critical minerals shaped African economies? | Regional Integration, Trade & Continental Geopolitics | What are the institutional opportunities and structural hurdles facing the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) and Regional Economic Communities (ECOWAS, SADC, EAC)?
