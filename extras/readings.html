Great Readings in African Studies

This curated bibliography presents foundational monographs, landmark essays, and paradigm-shifting interventions across African Studies. Rather than introductory textbooks, these works represent the core intellectual debates, methodological breakthroughs, and theoretical frameworks that have defined the discipline for independent scholars.
