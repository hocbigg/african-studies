Summary

The African Studies curriculum is a comprehensive education in African Studies, designed for self-directed study using online materials. This curriculum emphasizes interdisciplinary approaches, covering the African continent's histories, cultures, societies, politics, and global connections, including diaspora elements where relevant to continental dynamics.

How to contribute

Communities

  • You can also interact through GitHub issues. If there is a problem with a course, or a change needs to be made to the curriculum, this is the place to start the conversation. Read more here.

Curriculum

Scope & Epistemologies of African Studies

The African Experience: An Introduction (Vincent B. Khapoya / Routledge) - A standard introductory college textbook surveying African geography, historical epochs, sociocultural systems, and modern politics.

The Invention of Africa: Gnosis, Philosophy, and the Order of Knowledge (V. Y. Mudimbe / Indiana University Press) - A seminal theoretical critique examining how Western epistemological frameworks historically constructed the concept of Africa.

Africa and the Politics of Knowledge (MIT OpenCourseWare / Prof. M. Amah Edoh) - An open undergraduate course with syllabus, lecture notes, and readings on how knowledge about the continent is generated, consumed, and contested.

African Historiography & Methodologies

Oral Tradition as History (Jan Vansina / University of Wisconsin Press) - The definitive methodological treatise establishing critical standards for collecting, interpreting, and validating African oral traditions.

General History of Africa, Vol. 1: Methodology and African Prehistory (UNESCO / Ed. Joseph Ki-Zerbo) - A comprehensive, open-access scholarly volume covering African historical methods, oral history, linguistics, and archaeology from an African perspective.

The History of Africa with Zeinab Badawi (BBC News Africa / YouTube) - A 20-part documentary series produced alongside UNESCO that explores historical sites across the continent and interviews African specialists on local historical evidence.

African Physical & Human Geography

Africa South of the Sahara: A Geographical Interpretation (Robert Stock / Guilford Press) - An authoritative textbook that examines the continent's physical topography, climate zones, environmental history, and human geography.

World Regional Geography: Sub-Saharan Africa (Caitlin Finlayson / Open Textbook Library) - An open-access college textbook module analyzing environmental regimes, urbanization, and spatial organization across the continent.

Human Origins & African Prehistory

African Civilizations: An Archaeological Perspective (Graham Connah / Cambridge University Press) - An accessible introduction to the material culture, early agricultural systems, metallurgy, and urban settlement developments in prehistoric Africa.

The Oxford Handbook of African Archaeology (Eds. Peter Mitchell & Paul Lane / Oxford University Press) - A foundational handbook providing regional overviews of early hominin evolution, Stone Age tool traditions, and rock art.

Archaeology and Heritage of Africa (Coursera / Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona & DAI) - A free audit-mode MOOC that reviews hominin fossil discovery sites, early human adaptations, and cultural heritage management across the continent.

African Languages, Peoples & Historical Migrations

African Languages: An Introduction (Eds. Bernd Heine & Derek Nurse / Cambridge University Press) - A foundational linguistics textbook introducing Africa's major language phyla, structural classifications, and sociolinguistic environments.

The Civilizations of Africa: A History to 1800 (Christopher Ehret / University of Virginia Press) - A canonical historical study combining comparative linguistics, archaeology, and oral traditions to trace early continental demographic movements and the Bantu expansions.

Early Civilizations, States & Trans-Regional Trade

History of Africa (Kevin Shillington / Bloomsbury Academic) - A comprehensive single-volume undergraduate history covering Nubia, Aksum, the Swahili coast, Great Zimbabwe, and trans-Saharan trading empires.

Sundiata: An Epic of Old Mali (D. T. Niane / Pearson) - The canonical transcription of the Mandinka oral epic, providing direct insight into medieval West African state formation, kingship, and oral historiography.

Africa's Great Civilizations (PBS / Henry Louis Gates, Jr.) - A six-part visual documentary series surveying the intellectual, political, and material contributions of classical and medieval African polities.

Slavery, Slave Trades & African Diasporas

Transformations in Slavery: A History of Slavery in Africa (Paul E. Lovejoy / Cambridge University Press) - The standard historical study of internal African servitude systems and their transformation through external trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean, and Atlantic trades.

How Europe Underdeveloped Africa (Walter Rodney / Verso Books) - A foundational political economy analysis detailing how commercial extraction and the slave trade altered African demographics and structural development.

Reversing Sail: A History of the African Diaspora (Michael A. Gomez / Cambridge University Press) - An introductory survey tracking the global dispersal of African peoples and cultures from antiquity through the Atlantic trade into the modern world.

European Imperialism & Colonial Rule (1884–1960)

African Perspectives on Colonialism (A. Adu Boahen / Johns Hopkins University Press) - A concise, seminal study analyzing how African polities understood, negotiated, and militarily resisted European conquest during the Scramble for Africa.

Citizen and Subject: Contemporary Africa and the Legacy of Late Colonialism (Mahmood Mamdani / Princeton University Press) - A structural institutional critique showing how colonial direct and indirect rule bifurcated African legal orders into civic and customary domains.

Modern African History (MIT OpenCourseWare / Prof. Kenda Mutongi) - An open university course detailing the partition of Africa, colonial administrative strategies, forced labor regimes, and social changes under empire.

African Resistance, Nationalisms & Decolonization

The Wretched of the Earth (Frantz Fanon / Grove Press) - A foundational anti-colonial treatise analyzing the psychological dynamics, structural violence, and political trajectory of national liberation movements.

Africa since 1940: The Past of the Present (Frederick Cooper / Cambridge University Press) - An authoritative historical analysis tracing mid-twentieth-century labor strikes, the rise of mass nationalist movements, and the limits of the decolonization process.

Post-Colonial Politics & Governance

An Introduction to African Politics (Alex Thomson / Routledge) - The standard undergraduate textbook introducing clientelism, neopatrimonialism, military coups, civil conflict, and democratic transition.

The State in Africa: The Politics of the Belly (Jean-François Bayart / Polity Press) - A seminal political sociology text demonstrating how informal patronage networks and extraversion shape contemporary state power beneath formal institutional structures.

The State of Africa: A History of the Continent Since Independence (Martin Meredith / Simon & Schuster) - A comprehensive narrative account chronicling the political, institutional, and leadership trajectories of African states from independence into the twenty-first century.

Democracy and Development: Perspectives from Africa (MITx / edX / Prof. Evan Lieberman) - A free audit-mode course featuring video lectures and interviews with African scholars on democratic institutions, electoral processes, and governance.

Political Economy of African Development

Democracy and Development in Africa (Claude Ake / Brookings Institution Press) - A classic study arguing that developmental failure stems from authoritarian post-colonial governance structures rather than flawed technical policy prescriptions.

Poor Numbers: How We Are Misled by African Development Statistics and What to Do about It (Morten Jerven / Cornell University Press) - An essential critique documenting capacity deficits, methodology issues, and political distortion in African GDP and macroeconomic data.

Our Continent, Our Future: African Perspectives on Structural Adjustment (Thandika Mkandawire & Charles C. Soludo / IDRC) - An open-access scholarly assessment by leading African economists evaluating the impact of IMF and World Bank Structural Adjustment Programs.

Pan-Africanism & African International Relations

Africa Must Unite (Kwame Nkrumah / Panaf Books) - The classic political manifesto outlining the historical case for continental political union, collective economic planning, and regional defense against neo-colonialism.

Africa and the International System: The Politics of State Survival (Christopher Clapham / Cambridge University Press) - A seminal international relations textbook explaining how weak and vulnerable African states assert agency and navigate the global political order.

African Religions & Philosophies

African Religions and Philosophy (John S. Mbiti / Heinemann) - The pioneering academic study examining indigenous metaphysical cosmologies, notions of ontological time, and traditional religious practices across the continent.

A Companion to African Philosophy (Ed. Kwasi Wiredu / Wiley-Blackwell) - A definitive reference volume collecting essays on indigenous epistemology, sage philosophy, moral theories, and contemporary African intellectual discourse.

African Ethics (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy) - A peer-reviewed open-access primer covering communitarianism, humanism, personhood, and Ubuntu ethics.

African Traditional Religions (Harvard Pluralism Project) - An open educational overview detailing indigenous belief systems, rituals, and their historic coexistence with African Christianity and Islam.

African Literature, Orature & Expressive Arts

Things Fall Apart (Chinua Achebe / Anchor Books) - The canonical African novel portraying pre-colonial Igbo community life and the disruptive cultural and structural impact of British colonial arrival.

Decolonising the Mind: The Politics of Language in African Literature (Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o / James Currey) - A foundational essay collection advocating for linguistic decolonization and the revitalization of indigenous African languages in literature and theatre.

Global Africa: Creative Cultures (MIT OpenCourseWare / Prof. M. Amah Edoh) - An open university course that surveys African cinema, popular music, literary traditions, and visual arts within a global cultural marketplace.

The Danger of a Single Story (Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie / TED) - A recorded lecture on narrative power, cultural representation, and the necessity of multiple perspectives when interpreting African realities.

Kinship, Gender & African Feminisms

African Gender Studies: A Reader (Ed. Oyèrónkẹ́ Oyěwùmí / Palgrave Macmillan) - A comprehensive reader bringing together classic essays on African family systems, motherhood, female political authority, and distinct gender roles.

The Invention of Women: Making an African Sense of Western Gender Discourses (Oyèrónkẹ́ Oyěwùmí / University of Minnesota Press) - A landmark sociological work arguing that biological sex was not an organizing social principle in pre-colonial Yoruba society prior to colonial imposition.

Decolonization and Afro-Feminism (Sylvia Tamale / Daraja Press) - An open-access theoretical study applying an Afro-feminist and decolonial framework to African law, human rights, sexuality, and institutional power.

Urbanization, Demographics & Social Change

New Urban Worlds: Inhabiting Dissonant Times (AbdouMaliq Simone & Edgar Pieterse / Polity Press) - A spatial sociological study analyzing informal livelihood strategies, everyday infrastructure, and youth survival across rapid African urbanization.

The Time of Youth: Work, Social Change, and Politics in Africa (Alcinda Honwana / Kumarian Press) - A key sociological study examining the demographic youth bulge, the socioeconomic limbo of "waithood," and youth political mobilization.

Why Study African Cities? (Roderick McIntosh / Yale University) - A recorded university lecture reviewing the deep archaeological and historical roots of African urban forms and indigenous urban planning traditions.
