Resources

Historical Archives & Primary Source Repositories

This section provides access to digitized historical documents, maritime slave trade manifests, Cold War diplomatic files, and translated primary accounts of African history.

  • Slave Voyages (Rice University & Hutchins Center) - An open-access quantitative database and interactive map tracking ship manifests, demographic data, and routes across the Trans-Atlantic, Intra-American, and Indian Ocean slave trades.
  • South African History Online (SAHO) - An independent, open digital history archive housing primary documents, oral testimonies, biographies, and historical timelines centered on Southern African liberation struggles.
  • Internet African History Sourcebook (Fordham University / Paul Halsall) - A structured collection of public-domain and copy-permitted historical texts spanning ancient Nubia, medieval travelers' accounts, and anti-colonial manifestos.
  • Wilson Center Digital Archive: Cold War in Africa (Woodrow Wilson Center) - A searchable repository of declassified diplomatic cables, intelligence briefs, and meeting minutes documenting superpower rivalries and liberation movements across Africa.
  • Endangered Archives Programme: Africa (British Library & Arcadia) - A vast collection of digitized, at-risk historical manuscripts, ecclesiastical records, private correspondence, and rare newspapers from across the continent.

Governance, Public Opinion & Political Data

This section features pan-African survey datasets, governance scorecards, and continental legal frameworks measuring institutional performance and citizen attitudes.

  • Afrobarometer (Afrobarometer Network) - An independent pan-African research network providing open-access microdata, survey instruments, and country scorecards tracking African public opinion on democracy, economy, and society.
  • Ibrahim Index of African Governance (Mo Ibrahim Foundation) - A comprehensive quantitative assessment measuring public goods delivery, security, rule of law, and human development across all 54 African nations.
  • African Union Treaties, Conventions and Protocols (African Union) - The official legal portal providing full texts, ratification tables, and status lists for all continental charters, including the Banjul Charter and the Maputo Protocol.

Conflict Monitoring & Security Analysis

This section aggregates near-real-time political violence tracking, incident mapping, and research on African peace architectures.

  • Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project (ACLED) - A disaggregated crisis-mapping database documenting political violence, demonstrations, fatalities, and non-state armed actor activity across the African continent.
  • Institute for Security Studies (ISS Africa) - An applied policy research portal publishing security briefs, peace mission assessments, and risk analysis regarding regional conflict systems and terrorism.

Trade, Economics & Development Statistics

This section offers macroeconomic datasets, trade law policy briefs, and regional integration monitors across continental African economies.

  • Trade Law Centre (tralac) - A trade governance knowledge portal offering legal guides, tariff trackers, working papers, and implementation updates on the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA).
  • ECA Institutional Repository (United Nations Economic Commission for Africa) - The primary open repository for UN-ECA research, housing socioeconomic surveys, trade analysis, and macroeconomic policy publications from 1958 to the present.
  • Open Data for Africa (African Development Bank Group) - An interactive data portal offering standardized statistical indicators on national accounts, infrastructure indices, poverty metrics, and agricultural production across Africa.

Scholarly Portals & Africana Bibliographies

This section indexes peer-reviewed open-access journals, pan-African social science working papers, and specialized citation tools designed to center African knowledge production.

  • African Journals OnLine (AJOL) - The world's largest digital library of peer-reviewed, African-published academic journals, providing thousands of open-access articles across humanities and social sciences.
  • AfricaBib (African Studies Centre Leiden) - A free bibliographic search engine containing over 250,000 indexed records covering Africana periodical literature, African women's studies, and regional history.
  • CODESRIA Publications & Open Library (Council for the Development of Social Science Research in Africa) - The flagship open-access monograph and working-paper repository of Africa's premier social science council, focusing on decolonial theory and political economy.
  • Africa Portal (South African Institute of International Affairs & CIGI) - A policy research repository hosting over 5,000 open-access research reports, policy briefs, and monographs from prominent continental think tanks.

Linguistics, Oral Traditions & Cultural Heritage

This section brings together language typology databases, recorded oral literature archives, and visual material culture collections.

  • World Atlas of Language Structures Online: Africa (Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology) - A structural linguistic atlas providing interactive geographic mapping and phonological/grammatical properties for hundreds of indigenous African languages.
  • Glottolog (Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology) - A comprehensive bibliographic database and genealogical classification engine tracking language families, dialects, and reference grammars across Africa.
  • World Oral Literature Project (University of Cambridge) - An open multimedia archive dedicated to documenting, transcribing, and preserving endangered oral poetry, songs, and historical narratives.
  • Smithsonian National Museum of African Art Collections (Smithsonian Institution) - A searchable digital collections database featuring high-resolution images, provenance data, and curatorial notes for thousands of historical and contemporary African artworks.
